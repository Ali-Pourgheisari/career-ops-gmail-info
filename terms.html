Personal installation

Career Ops Gmail is a personal, locally operated integration for importing job-alert links from an account the operator is authorized to access. This website is informational and does not offer a public Gmail connection service.

Authorization and control

Only connect an account you control or are authorized to access. Review Google's consent screen before authorizing the integration. You can stop the importer and revoke Google access at any time.

Job information

Emails and job listings may be incomplete, duplicated, outdated, or unavailable. Review job details on the employer's website before acting. Importing a link does not confirm that a position is open and does not submit an application.

Availability

This personal tool is provided as available, without a guarantee of uninterrupted operation, delivery of every alert, or continued compatibility with third-party services. Google and GitHub remain governed by their respective terms.

Privacy and contact

See the privacy policy for data access, storage, and revocation information. Contact the operator through the repository issue tracker; do not include private emails or credentials.
